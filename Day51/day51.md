# Final Project

## AI Interview Coach

**AI Interview Coach** is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers improve their interview performance through personalized mock interviews and structured AI feedback.

Users can select a target role such as Software Engineer, Data Analyst, Data Scientist, or Frontend Developer. They can optionally provide a Job Description or upload their resume to receive customized interview questions.

For every response, the AI provides:

* Overall Score
* Strengths
* Areas for Improvement
* Improved Sample Answer
* Structure & Clarity Feedback
* Technical Depth Feedback
* Keyword Analysis
* Confidence Rating

The application also stores previous interview sessions and provides a dashboard to monitor overall progress over time.
